Relating Cancer Rates to Nitrate Levels from Wells in Wisconsin

Information about the application

This application highlights the relationship between cancer rate and nitrate level for census tracts in Wisconsin.
This is accomplished using Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW), Zonal Statistics, and Generalized Linear Regression (GLR).
Users can input the Distance Decay Coefficient, which changes the analysis by decreasing the influence of distant points.
The higher the coefficient, the lower the influence.
The most reasonable results will be obtained from a Distance Decay coefficient ranging from 1 - 3.
